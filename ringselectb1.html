Follow your Heart.

You are walking down central park late in the evening. You are overloaded with midterms and pending shifts. You decide to distance yourself from crowded areas that you walk towards a more secluded area of the park. Upon walking under a long dark bridge a small thud is heard coming from the end. At first you are nervous, but with your current events and stress you aren't in a right state of mind to make proper choices. So you march forward towards the thud you heard earlier. Suddenly, the dark bridge began to emit a light. A yellow and a orange glow that pulled you towards it. The glowing lights fly towards you and hover in front of you. The world becomes silent, and yet you hear something or someone whispering to you. You begin to understand that the whispering comes from the two glowing rings, "join ussss."
